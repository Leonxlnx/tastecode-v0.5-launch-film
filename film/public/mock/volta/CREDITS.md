# Volta mock site: credits

Placeholder footage for the launch film. Volta is a fictional brand; every model name, price, specification, address and date on the page is invented.

## Photograph

All machine pictures (`site/img/bike.png`, `frame.png`, `lamp.png`, `wheel.png`, `tail.png`) are edited from one photograph:

- "Harley Davidson LiveWire right noBG" by Auge=mit, CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons
- Source page: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Harley_Davidson_LiveWire_right_noBG.jpg
- Licence: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0
- Changes: background and ground shadow removed, orange paint moved to yellow, maker's badge and caliper lettering painted out, cropped.
- The edited versions fall under the same CC BY-SA 3.0 licence. The credit also sits in the page footer, under "Image credits".

The maker's marks are removed, but the shape is still the photographed production motorcycle, so the credit above stays with every copy of these images.
